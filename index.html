Emergency Formula Cheatsheet

Module 1: Coplanar Forces & Centroid

• Resultant: $R = \sqrt{(\sum F_x)^2 + (\sum F_y)^2}$

• Angle: $\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{|\sum F_y|}{|\sum F_x|}\right)$

• Varignon's Theorem: $R \times d = \sum M_A$

• Centroid: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum A_i x_i}{\sum A_i}, \quad \bar{y} = \frac{\sum A_i y_i}{\sum A_i}$

• Semicircle centroid from base: $y = \frac{4R}{3\pi}$

Module 2: Equilibrium & Beams

• Equilibrium: $\sum F_x = 0, \; \sum F_y = 0, \; \sum M = 0$

• Lami's Theorem: $\frac{F_1}{\sin \alpha} = \frac{F_2}{\sin \beta} = \frac{F_3}{\sin \gamma}$

• UDL load: $W = w \times L$ at midpoint $L/2$

• UVL load: $W = \frac{1}{2} w L$ at $2/3 L$ from zero-vertex

Module 3: Friction & Wedges

• Friction: $F_s = \mu N$ (opposes relative motion)

• Angle of friction: $\tan \phi = \mu$

• Angle of Repose: $\theta = \phi$

• Tipping vs Slipping: $P_{\text{slip}} = \mu W$ vs $P_{\text{tip}} = W(b/2)/h$

Module 4: Kinematics of Particle

• $v = u + at, \quad s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2, \quad v^2 = u^2 + 2as$

• Variable acceleration: $s = \int v dt, \quad v = \int a dt$

• Projectile Trajectory: $y = x\tan\theta - \frac{gx^2}{2u^2\cos^2\theta}$

• $u_x = u\cos\theta, \; u_y = u\sin\theta$

Module 5: Rigid Body & ICR

• Velocity at point P: $v_P = \omega \times (IP)$

• Angular velocity: $\omega = \frac{v_A}{IA} = \frac{v_B}{IB}$

• Slider crank: draw $\perp$ to $v_A$ and vertical $\perp$ to $v_B$ to find ICR

Module 6: Kinetics & D'Alembert's

• D'Alembert's: $\sum F - ma = 0$ (Inertia force $F_i = -ma$)

• Elevator upward: $T = m(g + a)$

• Elevator downward: $T = m(g - a)$

• Block on incline: $a = g(\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta)$
